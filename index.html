How to run Experiment 001

  1. Place the iPad flat on the same stable surface. Remove any case if safe and practical; do not change setup midway.
  2. Enable microphone and motion permissions. Keep the room quiet.
  3. Record at least five baseline runs with nothing on the iPad.
  4. Only if safe, repeat with a small, broad, soft-bottomed object of independently known mass. Avoid point loads and anything that can scratch or crack the glass.
  5. Export the CSV and compare runs. Look for repeatable differences larger than baseline variation.
Important interpretation: the browser cannot read raw touchscreen capacitance, display strain, or a direct static force signal. A repeatable acoustic/motion signature is only an indirect proxy. If the signal does not separate from baseline noise, this hardware-free approach cannot meet the target.
Runs are kept only in this page's memory until exported. Microphone access requires a secure context (HTTPS or localhost) and your permission. This prototype does not upload recordings.
